Overview

About this guide

This Reader's Guide is designed to enrich your reading experience without spoiling what comes next. Alongside spoiler-safe chapter notes, you'll find companion resources that explore the novel's characters, technology, themes, organizations, places, and timeline as they are introduced.

Rather than explaining everything at once, the guide grows alongside the novel, preserving its mystery while offering additional context for curious readers.
